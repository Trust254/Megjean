My Apology

Dear Megjean,

I owe you an apology. Not a quick “sorry” that disappears after a few seconds, but a real explanation from the heart.

I disappeared after work and life became complicated. Instead of explaining myself properly, I became quiet. I know that silence can hurt, especially when someone deserves an explanation.

The truth is that I never stopped appreciating you. There was something about you that stayed in my mind — your beautiful heart, your smile, your presence and the way you carry yourself.

You are beautiful, yes, but your beauty is not the only thing that caught my attention. It is your heart. Your warmth. Your confidence. The way you can be soft and strong at the same time.

And yes... I would be lying if I said I never noticed those beautiful curves too. ❤️ You have a kind of beauty that is difficult to forget.

I am not writing this to pressure you or ask you to forget everything. I simply wanted you to know what I should have told you much earlier.

If there is still a small space in your heart where we can talk again, I would be grateful for the chance.

Maybe we don't have to recreate the past. Maybe we can simply start a better chapter.

From someone who should have said this sooner... ❤️
